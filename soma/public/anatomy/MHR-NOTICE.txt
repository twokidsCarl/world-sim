The following data in this directory are derived from Momentum Human Rig (MHR) v1.0.1:
  the piece of the skin parts (FJ2810, SOMA_skin in integumentary.glb) of male/ and female/ marked as MHR,
  the skin weights in rig.json / rig.bin, the shape fields in shape.json / shape.bin,
  look/skin-uv.json / skin-uv.bin (the UV layout).

改动 / changes (statement under section 4(b) of the Apache License 2.0):
  身份系数、骨架缩放和姿势拟合到 soma 的人体；逐顶点修正（男性朝 BodyParts3D 的皮肤，两个人体都向外包住内部结构）；
  切掉部分皮肤并换上别的来源的移植片（脚趾、外生殖器），剪开眼和口；网格简化；UV 搬到简化后的网格上，接缝处拆分顶点。
  identity, skeleton scale and pose fitted to the soma bodies; per-vertex corrections (towards the BodyParts3D skin for
  the male, outwards to enclose the internal parts for both); pieces of skin cut out and replaced by grafts from other
  sources (toes, external genitalia), eyes and mouth opened; mesh simplification; the UVs carried over to the simplified
  mesh, with vertices split at the seams.
