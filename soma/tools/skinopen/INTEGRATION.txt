0. 怎么重跑（皮肤包重新发布之后）
------------------------------------------------------------------------
在 soma/ 目录下：

    tools/skinopen/run.sh male "" -next
    tools/skinopen/run.sh female "" -next

（第三个参数是输出目录的后缀。不带后缀会写已发布的目录，脚本会拒绝，除非设了 SKINOPEN_WRITE_SHIPPED=1；见第 13 节。
带移植步骤的写法：tools/skinopen/run.sh male genital,toes -next。）
每次都从头生成，结果是确定的（同样的输入，逐字节相同的输出）。三步：
  1) bun tools/skinopen/export-deep.ts <body> data/raw/bodyshape/out/<body>-skin-open/deep-face.json
     从 public/anatomy/<body>/ 导出面部深层部件和颞下颌关节（只读）。
  2) python tools/skinopen/open_skin.py data/raw/bodyshape/out/<body>-skin
     读原包，写 data/raw/bodyshape/out/<body>-skin-open/。原包一个字节也不动。
  3) bun tools/skinopen/render.ts <body>     写 <body>-skin-open/renders/*.png。
重跑之后请看：
  · 退出码非 0 = 自检失败或找不到线（错误信息会说是哪一条）。
  · <body>-skin-open/open-report.json 的 checks 应全为 true，result.boundaryLoops 应为两个环（两眼内卷的内缘）。
  · renders/rest-mouthFront.png 左右两格应当看不出差别；run.sh 会打印静止时口区的像素差（应为 0 或个位数）。
顺序：export-deep 读的是当前的 public/anatomy/<body>/。深层部件只决定口腔袋的大小和睑缘内卷的长度，
不决定剪在哪里；但若人体在皮肤重新发布之后又重建过，请再跑一次。
工具不依赖某一次拟合：线都从几何、manifest 的 face.regions（lips、lidsL、lidsR）和 joints.json 里找。
它假定：两唇之间有 MHR 的那个内折口袋；两睑缘之间有一条没有内部顶点的三角形带。
皮肤作者若改了这两处的拓扑，工具会报错而不是悄悄出错。

1. 方法（简述）
------------------------------------------------------------------------
口：MHR 两唇之间是一个向内折的口袋（深 2.7–6 mm，两壁相贴并略有相穿），袋底是一排顶点，从口角到口角。
    袋底顶点的相邻面法线几乎相对，按“相邻面法线最大夹角 > 90°”找到袋底，两端即口角，其间走最短路。
    沿袋底剪开：路径内部顶点复制一份，上壁保留原下标，下壁用新下标（接在文件末尾）。
    口袋的两壁就是上、下唇的内面，所以没有另加唇的内卷。静止时外观与原皮肤逐像素相同。
眼：原皮肤的眼是睁开的；上下睑缘之间由一条之字形三角形带封住（每眼 62 / 64 个三角形，平的，不是眼球形）。
    把这条带删掉，睑裂就是洞，露出解剖数据里真正的眼球。沿洞口加一圈朝向眼球中心的内卷（0.5–5 mm）。
口腔袋：接在两条唇缘后面的两片壳（上半随颅，下半随下颌），在咬合平面上的一条缝处相接；
    大小取牙、牙龈、舌的包围盒加 3 mm；离闭合皮肤不到 3 mm 的顶点被推回体内。它和内卷都是构造物，不是解剖。
权重：在剪开后的网格上解调和“侧别场”（上唇缘 +1，下唇缘 −1）。目标下颌份额在 |场| ≥ 0.8 处取 0 / 1；
    口裂周围 5 mm（沿表面）内严格取目标值，到 16 mm 处平滑回到原权重。眼睑的权重不动。
其余逐顶点文件：按 source-vertex.u32 扩展；前 73,639 项与原文件逐位相同。

2. 包里有什么（<body>-skin-open/）
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  skin.obj            开口后的皮肤。顶点 0…73,638 与原皮肤相同、顺序相同，新顶点接在后面。
                      分组（g 行）：skin、lidRimL、lidRimR、oralPouchUpper、oralPouchLower。不闭合（两个边界环）。
  skin-closed.obj     原皮肤，逐字节照抄，闭合。凡是需要闭合曲面的程序用它。
  weights.bin         默认权重：口裂处精确分开直到口角。记录 (uint32 顶点, uint32 关节, float32 权重)，小端。
  weights-corner-taper.bin   另一份权重：从每个口角起 6 mm 弧长内渐入。与 weights.bin 二选一（见第 5 节）。
  joints.json         照抄。
  *.f32               全部逐顶点场，已扩展到新顶点数。
  shape-fields.json   照抄，vertices 改为新顶点数。
  vertex-tags.u8      每个顶点一个字节，见下表。
  source-vertex.u32   每个顶点一个 uint32：它的逐顶点数据取自 skin-closed.obj 的哪个顶点。
                      前 73,639 个就是自己；下唇缘的复制顶点 = 它的孪生顶点；内卷顶点 = 所属的睑缘顶点；
                      口腔袋顶点 = 同一列的上唇缘顶点。这不是“位置最近的顶点”，只是数据的来源。
  manifest.json       原 manifest 加上 face.margins / sheets / constructionAids / added / removedEyeMembrane /
                      topology（原来的在 topologyAsDelivered），以及 opened（修改声明、选项、数量、标签表）。
  open-report.json    本次运行的全部测量和自检。
  deep-face.json      导出的深层部件（可选的输入）。
  LICENSE-MHR.txt、NOTICE-MHR.txt   必须随包一起走（Apache-2.0）；修改声明在 manifest.opened.modification。

vertex-tags.u8 的取值：
   0  skin              普通皮肤
   1  upperLip          上唇缘（路径内部顶点，原下标）
   2  lowerLip          下唇缘（复制出来的顶点，新下标）
   3  mouthCorner       口角（上下唇缘共用）
   4  upperLidL         左眼上睑缘        5  lowerLidL   左眼下睑缘
   6  upperLidR         右眼上睑缘        7  lowerLidR   右眼下睑缘
  13  canthus           眦（上下睑缘共用的顶点，每眼两个）
   8  lidRimL           左眼内卷（构造物）  9  lidRimR     右眼内卷（构造物）
  10  oralPouchUpper    口腔袋上半（构造物）
  11  oralPouchLower    口腔袋下半（构造物）
  12  oralPouchSeam     口腔袋的缝（构造物）

3. manifest 格式（已定稿）
------------------------------------------------------------------------
下标都是 skin.obj 的顺序。
  face.margins
    upperLip、lowerLip           从身体右侧口角（−X）到左侧口角（+X）。首尾是共用的口角顶点，
                                 其余顶点两两重合（位置相同、下标不同）。
    upperLidL、lowerLidL、upperLidR、lowerLidR   从内眦到外眦；两条链在每个眦共用一个顶点。
    mouthCornerR、mouthCornerL、medialCanthusL/R、lateralCanthusL/R   单个顶点。
  face.sheets                    剪口附近每个顶点在哪一片上（位置重合时不能靠位置区分）：
    mouth.upper / lower / lateral；eyeL、eyeR 的 upper / lower / canthi；rim；pouch。
  face.constructionAids
    lidRimL、lidRimR             { vertices, of }：of[i] 是 vertices[i] 所属的睑缘顶点。
    oralPouch                    { upper, lower, seam, rows, columns, layout }：按行存，第 r 行第 c 列 = r × columns + c；
                                 行从唇缘到缝；第 c 列属于 upperLip / lowerLip 去掉两端口角后的第 c 个顶点。
  face.added                     说明加了什么；都仍是皮肤网格的一部分，不是新的解剖部件。
  face.removedEyeMembrane        被删掉的三角形（在 skin-closed.obj 里仍在）。
  opened.cornerTaper             两份权重文件的说明。
任务书里的叫法 → manifest 里的名字：
  upperLipMargin / lowerLipMargin → upperLip / lowerLip
  upperLidMarginL/R、lowerLidMarginL/R → upperLidL/R、lowerLidL/R
  “口角” → mouthCornerR、mouthCornerL；“眦” → medialCanthusL/R、lateralCanthusL/R
  每顶点的 upper / lower 标签 → sheets.mouth、sheets.eyeL、sheets.eyeR 之下

4. 下游各程序要改什么
------------------------------------------------------------------------
根本的一条：唇缘的顶点成对重合，唇内面两壁相距 0.05–0.5 mm 且相穿；发布的 GLB 把位置量化到约 0.1 mm
（POSITION_BITS = 14）。所以在唇部，任何“按位置”的做法（按位置焊接、按最近点传递）都会出错：
口裂会被焊回去，上唇顶点会拿到下唇的权重。必须按下标对应。

tools/pipeline/run.ts、female.ts（装入）
  · 包目录改为 …-skin-open。显示用的皮肤部件取 skin.obj；需要闭合曲面的地方另读 skin-closed.obj。
  · lockVertices 里加上 vertex-tags.u8 非 0 的全部顶点（唇缘、睑缘、内卷、口腔袋不被简化）。
  · 保持“不焊接”；算法线时也不要按位置合并顶点。
  · 写一个下标对照文件：发布的皮肤的每个顶点 → 包里的顶点下标。simplifySkinKeepingFace 里已有 map，
    meshopt 不产生新顶点，所以每个发布顶点都有对应。下面各项都靠它。
tools/pipeline/skinPackage.ts
  · loadSkinPackage 同时返回闭合的那张网格（有 skin-closed.obj 就读它，否则就是 skin.obj）。
  · checkSkinFit、faceAgainstSkin、signedDistanceToSkin、notFittedAgainstSkin 用射线奇偶判断内外，必须用闭合网格。
    fit.closed 报告闭合网格的；开口皮肤的边界环另报（应为 2）。fitShipped 要么用简化后的闭合网格，要么跳过。
tools/pipeline/fat.ts
  · 传入闭合网格。开口皮肤会把口腔袋当成皮肤体素，skinTester.contains 在头内也不对。
tools/rig/field.ts（BodyField）
  · 传入闭合皮肤。口腔袋会在头内造出假的“皮肤壁”并关掉传播。
tools/rig/external.ts、bind.ts
  · 皮肤部件不要用 weld() 按位置合并；唯一顶点按下标对照文件定义。
  · transfer() 直接取 rows[包里的下标]，不用 closestFace。
  · generic 区域混合和邻域填补不要跨过重合的唇缘顶点对。
  · build.ts 对 skin.obj、weights.bin、joints.json、manifest.json 取哈希，新目录下照常可用。
tools/rig/shape-build.ts
  · pkgRel 改为 …-skin-open；shape-fat.f32、shape-limit.f32 按下标取，不用 closestFace。长度检查能通过。
tools/face/build.ts
  · 用 manifest.face.margins 和 sheets（经下标对照）代替在皮肤上量睑缘、唇线。
  · 去掉“口裂内陷”的替代做法和“上睑皮肤滑过那片皮肤”的闭眼；闭眼 = 上睑缘落到下睑缘上。
  · 内卷顶点跟随它的睑缘顶点（lidRim*.of）；口腔袋的每一列跟随它的唇缘顶点。
  · 口袋处皮肤法线不可靠（口袋是翻进去的）：分上下用 sheets，不用法线。
tools/skinmaps/mesh.ts
  · 涂色和测地距离跳过内卷（8、9）和口腔袋（10–12）顶点；射线和最近点查询忽略口腔袋的面。
  · 若体表图应在口裂处断开，就不要焊接唇缘顶点对。
src/（查看器）
  · 口腔袋的面画成深色、不透明，不参与点选和体表图；内卷按皮肤或黏膜画。
  · 可以背面剔除：绕向一致，口腔袋和内卷的正面朝向腔内。
tools/bodyshape（皮肤作者）
  · 不用改。重新发布后跑第 0 节的命令。

5. 口角：两种做法，以及建议
------------------------------------------------------------------------
weights.bin（默认）：上唇缘下颌份额恒为 0，下唇缘恒为 1，直到口角。分得精确，但只用关节摆姿势时，
  张口的两端是竖直的，口呈方形。
weights-corner-taper.bin：从每个口角起 6 mm 弧长内，上唇缘由 0.5 渐变到 0，下唇缘由 0.5 渐变到 1。
  张口 10° 时呈杏仁形，更自然；但这一段唇缘不再精确分开（每侧约 5–6 对顶点），张口 25° 时口角处有折皱和一个小缺口
  （见 renders/mouth-corner-taper.png：上排默认，下排渐入）。
建议：用默认的 weights.bin，口角的形状交给表情层（口角处上下唇的位移场）去做——精确分开的权重是干净的底子，
  表情层在上面加形状比在渐入的权重上修更容易。只有在表情层暂时不处理口角时，才用渐入的那份作为权宜之计。
两个文件格式和顶点编号完全相同，换文件即可，不必重跑本工具。别的渐入长度：--alt-corner-taper-mm N。

6. 深层的“唇”部件
------------------------------------------------------------------------
部件 id：男性 FJ2814，女性 W_FJ2814（名称 "Lip"，消化系统）。它是一整块，上下唇连在一起，正好位于口裂开口处。
皮肤一张口，它不动就会挡在开口里（我的渲染图里没有画它）。
建议分两种情况：
  · 画不透明皮肤时：隐藏这个部件。皮肤的唇和口袋的两壁已经表示了唇的外面和内面，再画它只会穿帮。
    这是最稳妥的做法，建议先这样做。
  · 皮肤隐藏或半透明时（肌肉视图、剥离）：它必须跟着动。按它自己的口裂（tools/face/build.ts 已经在量）
    把顶点分成上下两组，上组绑颅（skull），下组绑颞下颌关节（tmj），口角外侧混合；
    再跟随皮肤唇的切向位移场。它是一张连在一起的网格，口裂处的三角形会被拉长，除非也把它剪开——
    那是解剖数据的改动，不在本工具的范围内。

7. 仍未验证或需要留意的
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  · 没有经过管线、绑定、体型、表情各步的实际运行；只确认了 loadSkinPackage 能读开口后的目录。
  · meshopt 简化器遇到两个边界环和口腔袋时的行为没有试过。
  · 口角位置随拟合有约 1 mm 的出入：男女两条路径共用 43 个顶点，两端各差 1–3 个顶点，口角的顶点下标两体不同
    （四个眦的下标两体相同）。不要把口角下标写死，从 manifest 读。
  · 上唇离口裂 5 mm 以外仍带一些下颌份额（MHR 原来就给 0.15 左右），口角旁最多 0.34。
  · 口腔袋很粗：牙 97.5–99.8 %、牙龈 84–92 %、舌 65–71 % 的顶点在袋内（舌根从后面出去）。
    男性有 292 个袋顶点被推回才离皮肤 3 mm（女性 9 个）。
  · 皮肤上的眼与深层眼球没有对齐：男性睑裂中心比眼球中心低 3.5–3.8 mm（虹膜偏上，被上睑遮住一部分）；
    女性睑缘到眼球表面中位数 4.5 mm，内卷上限 5 mm，局部仍差约 0.5 mm。
  · 下睑的内卷从正面看是一条 2–3 mm 宽的粉色窄台。
  · MHR 唇内面两壁静止时相穿约 0.5 mm，本工具没有改；从外面看不到。
  · 渲染图里眼睑的动作只是测试用的粗糙位移，其中的折皱不是网格的问题。

8. 移植步骤（graft.py）：在开口之前、在闭合的皮肤上做
------------------------------------------------------------------------
先后次序：<body>-skin（原包，不动）→ graft.py 的各步 → <body>-skin-grafted/（仍然闭合）→ open_skin.py → <body>-skin-open/。
    tools/skinopen/run.sh male genital,toes
    tools/skinopen/run.sh female vulva,toes
第二个参数省略时不做移植，结果与以前相同。manifest.opened.steps 记录本包试过哪些步骤（最后一项是 open）；
真正接进去的在 manifest.grafted.grafts，定位了但没有接的在 manifest.grafted.fittedButNotGrafted（各带原因和文件名）。
此时 skin-closed.obj 是“移植之后、开口之前”的闭合皮肤；原皮肤另存为 skin-delivered.obj。

------------------------------------------------------------------------
本轮做完的：脚趾（两体）、失败测试、确定性核对、深层唇部件一分为二的兼容。没有做的：男性生殖器移植处的重做（第 2 项，只量了前提）、
女性外阴片收窄（第 3 项）。这两项仍是第 11 节所说的样子，代码没有动（genital.py、vulva.py 与上一轮逐字节相同）。
它取代的说法：第 10 节 “脚趾” 里的做法（每趾一个长度比例、逐顶点外推、弦高补推、相邻趾各留 0.3 mm、过渡带解位置的双调和方程）、
验收条件和 “结果 / 看图的结论” 两段；第 6 节 “它是一整块” 一句（见下）。

输出目录的后缀
  tools/skinopen/run.sh <body> <steps> <suffix>：第三个参数接在两个输出目录名之后，例如
      tools/skinopen/run.sh male genital,toes -next      →  male-skin-grafted-next/、male-skin-open-next/
      tools/skinopen/run.sh female vulva,toes -next
  不给后缀时写的是已发布的 …-skin-grafted/ 和 …-skin-open/（下游直接读它们）。本轮所有运行都带后缀；已发布的四个目录和两个原包在本轮前后
  逐文件哈希相同。其他脚本没有写死的输出路径：graft.py、open_skin.py、render.ts、render-graft.ts 只在不给 --out / 参数时才用默认目录，
  run.sh 总是给。manifest.json、open-report.json、open.log 里写有输入目录的名字，所以后缀不同的两次运行在这三个文件里差一个名字，别的逐字节相同。

脚趾（toes.py；graft.py 多了 rim_gap_mm、zipper_by_parameter、flip_folded_stitch_edges，只有 toes 用）
  上一轮折痕和碎片的来源，按查到的先后：
    1) 供体趾的中心线每 2 mm 一站，最后一站到不了趾蹼：最后一站与趾蹼之间（至多 2 mm）的顶点全被映到同一个弧长上，再被拉长 1.5 倍左右，
       趾根一圈被压扁（边长比 0.47），这就是趾根背面那道 “缝合处的台阶”——它不在缝合带上。现在最后一站正好在趾蹼。
       顺带的结果：供体游离趾的长度量得更准，第 3–5 趾的长度比例从 1.26–1.58 降到 1.22–1.46。
    2) 覆盖不足处逐顶点沿各自的方向外推，再对骨顶点最近的三角形三个顶点不加平滑地补推：趾尖的碎片和皱起的趾甲由此而来。
    3) 缝合带：供体片的边缘与洞口环在同一张曲面上相贴、相交，贪心的缝合缝出了翻过来的三角形（接入后每只脚 24–41 条折过 120° 的边，全在缝合带上）。
  现在的做法：
    · 长度分段：远段（趾尖到甲根后 1.5 mm：趾腹和趾甲）按该趾的粗细比例等比缩放，拉长全放在趾干上，两段之间在 4 mm 内缓变；趾蹼前后各 3 mm 内比例缓变到 1。
      远段 / 趾干的比例（大趾 … 小趾）：男 左 0.90/1.15, 1.01/1.00, 1.02/1.40, 1.06/1.74, 1.27/1.53；右 1.00/1.00, 0.99/1.01, 1.02/1.43, 1.11/1.74, 1.30/1.54。
      女 左 0.93/1.11, 1.02/0.99, 1.02/1.34, 1.05/1.63, 1.26/1.43；右 1.05/0.92, 1.04/1.01, 1.07/1.44, 1.14/1.70, 1.33/1.49。
      也就是说趾甲和趾腹不再被拉长，但第 3–5 趾的趾干被拉长 1.34–1.74 倍：供体与这两副骨的比例不合，这一点没有变，只是挪了地方。
    · 骨链的轴线和坐标架在 ±6 mm 内取平均（以前 ±3 mm）。过渡带解的是位移（P − 供体）的双调和方程，趾根最后 3 mm 一并解。
    · 趾尖帽（远段的全部顶点）作刚体运动：对 “各顶点各自外推后的位置” 求加权最小二乘的刚体变换，不放大、不变形；最后每个趾甲再对回供体趾甲的
      相似变换（残差 0；这一步修正掉的量：大趾 0.9–1.0 mm，其余 0.25–0.56 mm）。趾甲的顶点因此彼此不相对移动。
    · 其余顶点的外推量是标量场，在网格上取平滑的上包络（30 次，约 5 圈），方向场低通 8 次；被带动的顶点跟着带动它们的顶点的方向走。
      趾缝在趾根处留 2.6 mm、向远侧 6 mm 内收到 0.6 mm；分开邻趾时自己骨上的覆盖不低于 1.9 mm。
    · 折过 108° 的边就地松弛；高出供体细节层 0.8 mm 的顶点就地松弛。主循环 10 + 10 轮，然后是收尾（局部顶起、退出邻趾、松弛，至多 16 轮，
      取打分最高的一轮）和保底（骨仍离皮肤不到 0.7 mm 时直接顶出，至多 8 轮）。本轮四只脚都没有用到保底。
    · 缝合：供体片的边缘留在洞内、离洞口环至少 1.5 mm，沿环理顺 5 次；按 “沿洞口环的弧长参数” 归并缝合；缝合带内折过 100° 的对角线翻转。
  验收（全部通过才接入；每条的数都在 graft-report.json 的 toes.sides.* 和 toes.graft.* 里）：
    · 原有的：游离趾内趾骨覆盖骨干 ≥ 1.5 mm、骨端 ≥ 1.0 mm；相邻趾互不穿入；趾甲在远节趾骨背侧；片缘离皮肤中位数 ≤ 1 mm。
    · 新增：改动过的供体面上没有折过 120° 的边（foldedEdges）；游离趾上没有碎片（fragments：细节层 = 顶点高出自己低通 10 次后的曲面的量，
      与供体自己的细节层按粗细比例比较，高出 1 mm 以上的相连顶点算碎片；低 1 mm 以上的记为 hollows，不算失败——松弛会合理地抹掉供体的皱纹）；
      趾甲是供体趾甲的相似变换（残差 ≤ 0.001 mm）；接入后缝合带两条环上的顶点离原皮肤 ≤ 0.5 mm（seam.stepMm；缝合三角形重心的弦高另记，不作条件）；
      接入后这一片连同缝合带上折过 120° 的边不多于保底顶点上容许的数。
    · 改了的：接入后 “每节趾骨至少的覆盖” 由 1.0 mm 改为 0.5 mm（COVER_SADDLE_MM）。理由照实说：相邻近节趾骨表面相距只有 2.5–4.1 mm
      （报告里的 gapBetweenTheProximalPhalangesOfNeighbouringToesMm），趾根之间的鞍部从中间穿过，两边不可能各有 1 mm 以上而又不折。
      保底顶出的顶点上至多容许 4 条折边（LAST_RESORT_FOLDS）：那里 “骨不露出皮外” 与 “没有折边” 不能两全时取前者。本轮实际用到 0 条。

  上一版的毛病出在哪里：（1）构造面是把拟合皮肤的隆起抹平得到的，比扫描的会阴低 20–30 mm，阴囊颈和会阴缝的扫描面被它

  （两排位置相同的边界顶点），按洞用扇形去补，每条缝上就多出一片背靠背、折成 180° 的三角形。

    1) 拟合皮肤上要换掉的区域：离龟头、睾丸、附睾 120 mm 以内，与扫描相差超过 2.5 mm 的顶点，或在拟合皮肤自相交的
       三角形上的顶点（本包这里有 405 对，两大腿内侧在裆下互相穿过），或在拟合皮肤折过 60° 的边上的顶点（101 条）；
       连成的各块向外扩 1 圈、补洞。洞缘因此落在两张皮肤本来就吻合、拟合皮肤自己也平顺的地方。
    2) 扫描片的核：扫描上平滑后邻边平均长度不超过 5 mm、离深层部件 60 mm 以内的顶点，加上离深层部件 15 mm 以内的顶点；
       围着龟头和睾丸连通的一块，补洞；只取洞内、离洞缘至少 12 mm 的部分。供体的缝先焊上（286 个顶点并掉，位置不动），
       片内不再有任何补洞三角形。
    3) 片缘：核的边界附近 12 mm 内，扫描三角形的边先对半分到 3 mm（新点在原来的边上，曲面不变）；核的指示函数在扫描面上
       扩散（尺度 3 mm），沿 0.25 等值线把片切出来（三角形在自己的平面内被切；核上的顶点一个不丢）。片缘是一条光滑的闭合线，
       扫描顶点一个不动。

       （洞缘边长的中位数，即拟合皮肤当地的边长）；再光顺：只沿法向挪，取均匀权双拉普拉斯能量的最小值，扫描片和周围
       三圈拟合皮肤固定，中间以 0.01 的权重拉向扫描面（片缘到洞缘的前 0.7，到 0.95 渐减到 0）。

0. How to re-run (after a skin package is re-issued)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
From soma/:

    tools/skinopen/run.sh male "" -next
    tools/skinopen/run.sh female "" -next

(The third argument is a suffix for the output directories. Without it the shipped directories would be written, and
the script refuses unless SKINOPEN_WRITE_SHIPPED=1 is set; see section 13. With graft steps:
tools/skinopen/run.sh male genital,toes -next.)
Every run regenerates everything and is deterministic (same inputs, byte-identical outputs). Three steps:
  1) bun tools/skinopen/export-deep.ts <body> data/raw/bodyshape/out/<body>-skin-open/deep-face.json
     exports the deep facial parts and the temporomandibular joint from public/anatomy/<body>/ (read only).
  2) python tools/skinopen/open_skin.py data/raw/bodyshape/out/<body>-skin
     reads the delivered package and writes data/raw/bodyshape/out/<body>-skin-open/. The delivered
     package is not touched.
  3) bun tools/skinopen/render.ts <body>     writes <body>-skin-open/renders/*.png.
After a re-run, look at:
  · a non-zero exit status = a self-check failed or a line was not found (the message says which).
  · checks in <body>-skin-open/open-report.json must all be true, and result.boundaryLoops must hold two
    loops (the inner edges of the two lid rims).
  · the two panels of renders/rest-mouthFront.png must look the same; run.sh prints the pixel difference
    of the mouth region at rest (0 or a single digit).
Order: export-deep reads the current public/anatomy/<body>/. The deep parts only size the oral pouch and
the lid rims; they do not decide where the cuts go. If the body is rebuilt after the skin was re-issued,
run once more.
The tool does not depend on a particular fit: the lines come from geometry, from face.regions of the
manifest (lips, lidsL, lidsR) and from joints.json. It assumes that MHR's folded pocket lies between the
lips and that a strip of triangles without interior vertices lies between the lid margins. If the skin's
author changes either, the tool fails with an error rather than going wrong silently.

1. Method (brief)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mouth: between the lips MHR has a pocket folded inwards (2.7–6 mm deep, its walls against and slightly
    through each other) with one row of vertices at the bottom, from corner to corner. Round a bottom
    vertex the face normals are almost opposite; the bottom is found as "largest angle between incident
    face normals > 90°", its ends are the mouth corners, and a shortest path joins them. The cut runs along
    it: interior path vertices are duplicated, the upper wall keeps the original indices and the lower wall
    gets new ones (appended). The pocket's walls are the inner faces of the lips, so no further lip rim was
    added. At rest the result is pixel-identical to the delivered skin.
Eyes: the delivered skin has open eyes; a zigzag strip of triangles closes the space between the lid
    margins (62 / 64 triangles per eye, flat, not globe-shaped). The strip is removed, so each fissure is a
    hole showing the real eyeball of the anatomy data. A rim turned towards the globe centre (0.5–5 mm) is
    added round each hole.
Oral pouch: two shells behind the lip margins (upper with the skull, lower with the jaw) meeting at a seam
    in the occlusal plane; sized from the bounding box of teeth, gingiva and tongue plus 3 mm; vertices
    closer than 3 mm to the closed skin are pushed back in. Pouch and rims are construction aids, not anatomy.
Weights: a harmonic side field is solved on the cut mesh (+1 on the upper lip margin, −1 on the lower).
    The target jaw share is 0 / 1 where |field| ≥ 0.8; within 5 mm of the fissure (along the surface) the
    target is taken strictly, easing back to the delivered weights at 16 mm. Lid weights are unchanged.
Other per-vertex files are extended through source-vertex.u32; their first 73,639 entries are bit-identical.

2. What the package holds (<body>-skin-open/)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  skin.obj            the opened skin. Vertices 0…73,638 are those of the delivered skin in the same order;
                      new vertices follow. Groups (g lines): skin, lidRimL, lidRimR, oralPouchUpper,
                      oralPouchLower. Not closed (two boundary loops).
  skin-closed.obj     the delivered skin, byte for byte, closed. For every program that needs a closed surface.
  weights.bin         the default weights: exact split at the fissure up to the corners. Records
                      (uint32 vertex, uint32 joint, float32 weight), little endian.
  weights-corner-taper.bin   alternative weights: eased in over 6 mm of arc length from each mouth corner.
                      Use it or weights.bin, not both (section 5).
  joints.json         copied.
  *.f32               every per-vertex field, extended to the new vertex count.
  shape-fields.json   copied, with `vertices` set to the new count.
  vertex-tags.u8      one byte per vertex, table below.
  source-vertex.u32   one uint32 per vertex: the vertex of skin-closed.obj its per-vertex data come from.
                      The first 73,639 are themselves; a duplicated lower-lip vertex = its twin; a rim vertex
                      = the lid margin vertex it belongs to; a pouch vertex = the upper-lip margin vertex of
                      its column. It is the source of the data, not "the nearest vertex".
  manifest.json       the delivered manifest plus face.margins / sheets / constructionAids / added /
                      removedEyeMembrane / topology (the original under topologyAsDelivered) and `opened`
                      (statement of changes, options, counts, tag table).
  open-report.json    all measurements and self-checks of the run.
  deep-face.json      the exported deep parts (an optional input).
  LICENSE-MHR.txt, NOTICE-MHR.txt   must travel with the package (Apache-2.0); the statement of changes is
                      manifest.opened.modification.

Values of vertex-tags.u8:
   0  skin              ordinary skin
   1  upperLip          upper lip margin (interior path vertices, original indices)
   2  lowerLip          lower lip margin (the duplicates, new indices)
   3  mouthCorner       mouth corner (shared by both lip margins)
   4  upperLidL         left upper lid margin      5  lowerLidL   left lower lid margin
   6  upperLidR         right upper lid margin     7  lowerLidR   right lower lid margin
  13  canthus           canthus (the vertex shared by both lid margins; two per eye)
   8  lidRimL           left lid rim (construction)   9  lidRimR   right lid rim (construction)
  10  oralPouchUpper    upper half of the oral pouch (construction)
  11  oralPouchLower    lower half of the oral pouch (construction)
  12  oralPouchSeam     seam of the oral pouch (construction)

3. Manifest format (final)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
All indices are in the order of skin.obj.
  face.margins
    upperLip, lowerLip           from the body's right mouth corner (−X) to the left one (+X). First and last
                                 are the shared corner vertices; the others coincide in pairs (same position,
                                 different index).
    upperLidL, lowerLidL, upperLidR, lowerLidR   from the medial to the lateral canthus; the two chains of
                                 an eye share one vertex at each canthus.
    mouthCornerR, mouthCornerL, medialCanthusL/R, lateralCanthusL/R   single vertices.
  face.sheets                    which sheet each vertex near a cut lies on (position cannot tell coincident
                                 vertices apart): mouth.upper / lower / lateral; eyeL and eyeR with
                                 upper / lower / canthi; rim; pouch.
  face.constructionAids
    lidRimL, lidRimR             { vertices, of }: of[i] is the lid margin vertex vertices[i] belongs to.
    oralPouch                    { upper, lower, seam, rows, columns, layout }: stored row by row, row r
                                 column c = r × columns + c; rows run from the margin to the seam; column c
                                 belongs to vertex c of upperLip / lowerLip without the two corners.
  face.added                     says what was added; all of it stays part of the skin mesh, not a new
                                 anatomy part.
  face.removedEyeMembrane        the removed triangles (still present in skin-closed.obj).
  opened.cornerTaper             explains the two weights files.
Names in the task text → names in the manifest:
  upperLipMargin / lowerLipMargin → upperLip / lowerLip
  upperLidMarginL/R, lowerLidMarginL/R → upperLidL/R, lowerLidL/R
  "mouth corners" → mouthCornerR, mouthCornerL; "canthi" → medialCanthusL/R, lateralCanthusL/R
  a per-vertex upper / lower label → under sheets.mouth, sheets.eyeL, sheets.eyeR

4. What each downstream program must change
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The one fact behind all of it: lip margin vertices coincide in pairs, and the inner-lip walls lie
0.05–0.5 mm apart and pass through each other; the shipped GLB quantises positions to about 0.1 mm
(POSITION_BITS = 14). So at the lips anything done by position (welding by position, transfer by closest
point) goes wrong: the fissure is welded shut and upper-lip vertices receive lower-lip weights.
Correspondence must be by index.

tools/pipeline/run.ts, female.ts (install)
  · Point the package directory at …-skin-open. The displayed skin part takes skin.obj; wherever a closed
    surface is needed, read skin-closed.obj as well.
  · Add to lockVertices every vertex with a non-zero vertex-tags.u8 (margins, rims and pouch are not
    simplified).
  · Keep "no welding"; do not merge vertices by position when computing normals either.
  · Write an index sidecar: for every vertex of the shipped skin, its vertex index in the package.
    simplifySkinKeepingFace already has `map`, and meshopt creates no vertices, so every shipped vertex has
    one. Everything below depends on it.
tools/pipeline/skinPackage.ts
  · loadSkinPackage also returns the closed mesh (skin-closed.obj when present, else skin.obj).
  · checkSkinFit, faceAgainstSkin, signedDistanceToSkin and notFittedAgainstSkin decide inside / outside by
    ray parity and need the closed mesh. Report fit.closed for the closed mesh and the boundary loops of the
    opened skin separately (expected: 2). fitShipped needs a simplified closed mesh, or is skipped.
tools/pipeline/fat.ts
  · Pass the closed mesh. The opened skin would voxelise the pouch as skin, and skinTester.contains would be
    wrong inside the head.
tools/rig/field.ts (BodyField)
  · Pass the closed skin. The pouch would add false skin walls inside the head and close propagation.
tools/rig/external.ts, bind.ts
  · Do not weld() the skin part by position; unique vertices are defined by the index sidecar.
  · transfer() takes rows[package index] directly instead of closestFace.
  · The `generic` region blend and the neighbour fill must not reach across a coincident margin pair.
  · build.ts hashes skin.obj, weights.bin, joints.json and manifest.json; that works unchanged in the new
    directory.
tools/rig/shape-build.ts
  · pkgRel becomes …-skin-open; shape-fat.f32 and shape-limit.f32 are taken by index, not by closestFace.
    The length check passes.
tools/face/build.ts
  · Use manifest.face.margins and sheets (through the index sidecar) instead of measuring lid and lip lines
    on the skin.
  · Drop the "oral recess" stand-in and the "upper-lid skin slides over the patch" closure; eye closure =
    the upper lid margin comes down onto the lower one.
  · A rim vertex follows its lid margin vertex (lidRim*.of); each pouch column follows its lip margin vertex.
  · Skin normals are unreliable in the pocket (it is turned inside out): tell upper from lower by sheets,
    not by the normal.
tools/skinmaps/mesh.ts
  · Painting and geodesics skip rim (8, 9) and pouch (10–12) vertices; ray and closest-point queries ignore
    pouch faces.
  · Do not weld the lip margin pairs if a map is to stop at the fissure.
src/ (viewer)
  · Draw pouch faces dark and opaque and keep them out of picking and skin maps; draw the rims as skin or
    mucosa.
  · Back-face culling is safe: the winding is consistent, and pouch and rims face the cavity.
tools/bodyshape (the skin's author)
  · Nothing to change. After re-issuing, run the commands of section 0.

5. Mouth corners: two behaviours, and a recommendation
------------------------------------------------------------------------
weights.bin (default): jaw share 0 on the whole upper margin and 1 on the whole lower margin, right up to
  the corners. The split is exact, but posed by the joint alone the opening has vertical ends and the mouth
  looks square.
weights-corner-taper.bin: over 6 mm of arc length from each corner the upper margin goes from 0.5 to 0 and
  the lower from 0.5 to 1. At 10° of opening the mouth is almond-shaped and looks more natural; but over that
  stretch the margins are no longer split exactly (about 5–6 vertex pairs a side), and at 25° the corners
  crinkle and show a small notch (renders/mouth-corner-taper.png: default above, tapered below).
Recommendation: use the default weights.bin and let the expression layer shape the corners (displacement
  fields of the lips at the corners). Exactly split weights are a clean base, and adding shape on top of
  them is easier than repairing tapered weights. Use the tapered file only as a stopgap while the expression
  layer does nothing at the corners.
Both files have the same format and vertex numbering: swap the file, no re-run needed. For another taper
length: --alt-corner-taper-mm N.

6. The deep "Lip" part
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Part ids: male FJ2814, female W_FJ2814 (name "Lip", digestive system). It is one piece with both lips joined
and sits exactly in the oral opening. Once the skin's mouth opens, a Lip part that does not move blocks the
opening (it is left out of my renders).
Recommendation, in two cases:
  · When the skin is drawn opaque: hide this part. The skin's lips and the walls of the pocket already show
    the outer and inner faces of the lips, and drawing it as well can only show through. This is the safe
    choice and the one to do first.
  · When the skin is hidden or see-through (muscle view, peeling): it has to move. Split its vertices into an
    upper and a lower group along its own fissure (tools/face/build.ts already measures it), bind the upper
    group to the skull and the lower to the temporomandibular joint (tmj) with a blend lateral to the corners,
    and let it follow the tangential field of the skin lips. It is one connected mesh, so the triangles across
    its fissure will stretch unless it is cut too — that is a change to the anatomy data and outside this tool.

7. Still unverified, or to keep in mind
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  · Nothing was run through the pipeline, rig, shape or face builds; only that loadSkinPackage reads the
    opened directories was checked.
  · How meshopt's simplifier treats the two boundary loops and the pouch is untested.
  · The corner position depends on the fit by about 1 mm: the male and female paths share 43 vertices and
    differ by 1–3 vertices at each end, so the corner vertex indices differ between the bodies (the four
    canthus indices are the same in both). Read the corners from the manifest; do not hard-code them.
  · Beyond 5 mm from the fissure the upper lip still carries some jaw share (MHR itself gives about 0.15),
    up to 0.34 beside the corners.
  · The pouch is crude: 97.5–99.8 % of tooth vertices, 84–92 % of gingiva and 65–71 % of the tongue lie
    inside it (the tongue root leaves it at the back). In the male 292 pouch vertices had to be pushed back
    to keep 3 mm from the skin (9 in the female).
  · The eye of the skin and the deep globe are not aligned: in the male the centre of the fissure is
    3.5–3.8 mm below the globe centre (the iris sits high, partly under the upper lid); in the female the lid
    margins are a median 4.5 mm from the globe surface, the rim is capped at 5 mm, and about 0.5 mm of gap
    remains in places.
  · From the front the lower lid rim shows as a pink shelf 2–3 mm wide.
  · MHR's inner-lip walls pass through each other by about 0.5 mm at rest; this tool did not change that,
    and it cannot be seen from outside.
  · Lid movement in the renders is a crude test displacement; the creases there are not a fault of the mesh.

8. Graft steps (graft.py): before the openings, on the closed skin
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Order: <body>-skin (delivered, untouched) → the steps of graft.py → <body>-skin-grafted/ (still closed) →
open_skin.py → <body>-skin-open/.
    tools/skinopen/run.sh male genital,toes
    tools/skinopen/run.sh female vulva,toes
Without the second argument nothing is grafted and the result is as before. manifest.opened.steps records the
steps tried for this package (the last is `open`); what was really grafted is under manifest.grafted.grafts and
what was fitted but not grafted under manifest.grafted.fittedButNotGrafted (each with its reason and file names).
skin-closed.obj is then the closed skin after the grafts and before the openings; the delivered skin is kept as
skin-delivered.obj.

Steps and their state per body:
  male    genital  grafted. The skin of the external genitalia (penis, scrotum) comes from his own scanned skin
                   (BodyParts3D 4.0, outer wall of FJ2810; frame (x, z, −y) mm → m, verified against the deep parts).
                   The mound of the fitted skin is cut out (1,174 triangles, 528 vertices) and 3,521 vertices /
                   6,928 triangles are stitched in with 232 triangles. 3,334 vertices are where the scan has them
                   (tag graftScanned); 187 at the rim were moved to meet the fitted skin (tag graftBlend,
                   construction; moved by a median 0.5 mm, at most 9.8 mm). Ten small holes of the donor mesh were
                   closed with 32 triangles (construction). Licence: the OBJ headers say CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan and the
                   README says CC BY 4.0; both are quoted in the manifest and the difference is not resolved.
                   Weights: harmonic extension from the rim of the hole, turning into the pelvis alone (MHR joint
                   root) between 10 and 30 mm from the rim. Penis and scrotum move rigidly with the pelvis: they do
                   not hang, swing or give way to the thighs. Shape fields are extended harmonically likewise.
          toes     fitted, not grafted (below).
  female  vulva    fitted, not grafted. The perineal skin is the skin surface of the specimen of Wu et al. 2015
                   (46_Skin.obj, CC BY 4.0), mapped with the 16 kinds of installed WU_* structures against the raw
                   OBJ files (affine + smooth residual field; median residual per structure 0.6 mm). Two reasons
                   for not grafting: (1) the rim of the patch lies a median 17.6 mm from the present skin (up to

                   be raised to her perineal structures, which is the skin author's job; (2) with the flat cut
                   planes at both ends of the specimen slab removed, the patch is not a disc with one boundary
                   loop: 57 boundary edges lie on the caudal cut plane, i.e. the specimen's skin is truncated
                   inside the vulvar region and would have to be closed by construction first. The positioned and

          toes     fitted, not grafted (below).

                   rig side spreads the ball weight onto mtp1–5 by its nearest-joint rule.

Each entry of manifest.grafted.grafts[] has: name, provenance, source (with the licence note), patchVertices,

largest weight; its per-vertex data are a harmonic extension from the whole rim, not a copy of that vertex.

For the downstream tools:
