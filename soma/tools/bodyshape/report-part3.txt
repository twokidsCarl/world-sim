## 16. 按最终内部结构拟合的皮肤包（男 4bf4e6e4b29d70ad，女 031ce88117d19693）

两个包在 `data/raw/bodyshape/out/male-skin/` 和 `data/raw/bodyshape/out/female-skin/`，格式按 `tools/pipeline/skinPackage.ts`：`manifest.json`（`source` 四项、`fittedTo{body,key}`）和 `skin.obj`（MHR LOD0，73639 顶点、147274 三角形，单层，每条边恰为两个三角形共用）。

做法分两步：`fit_skin.py` 做参数拟合（男性贴合他原来的皮肤，女性按 1.606 m / BMI 21.6 的围度目标），`enclose_skin.py` 再把皮肤沿法线局部外推，直到全部深部结构（管线的 FAT_DEEP_TISSUES，不含皮肤系统）都在皮肤下至少 2.5 mm。

### 仍然不对的地方

- 女性：内部结构装不进 BMI 21.6 的皮肤。局部外推之后皮肤包住了一切，但体积 BMI 从 21.6 升到 24.7，颈、锁骨区、前臂、膝、手、眼眶周围能看到肌肉轮廓印出来的凹凸。这不是可以当成品的体表。
- 女性头仍后仰（随她的颅骨位置）。
- 女性乳房：HRA 部件不在深部结构之列，皮肤没有去包它们；现在 44–53 % 的腺体/脂肪/韧带采样点在皮肤外，乳头尖在皮肤外 25–29 mm。
- 男性：外生殖器被一个由光滑皮肤外推出的隆起盖住，外形是带棱面的鼓包，不是阴茎和阴囊的皮肤。
- 两者：足趾是一整块（MHR 没有分开的足趾）；手指末节处皮肤被外推了最多 39–44 mm。
- 修正后仍在皮肤外的采样点：男性 3 个部件（眼轮匝肌，最多 0.4 mm），女性 2 个（额肌，0.1 mm）。
- 我的胸围测量在这两张网格上不稳定（男性前后给出 1059 和 918 mm），不要用。
- 指纹无法由 anatomy.json 的包围盒复算（管线用的是未量化的骨网格）；`fittedTo.key` 是从 `pipeline-report.json` 照抄的，并检查了导出文件比 anatomy.json 新。

# Part 3 (English)

## 16. Skin packages fitted to the final internals (male 4bf4e6e4b29d70ad, female 031ce88117d19693)

The two packages are in `data/raw/bodyshape/out/male-skin/` and `data/raw/bodyshape/out/female-skin/`, in the format of `tools/pipeline/skinPackage.ts`: `manifest.json` (the four `source` fields and `fittedTo{body,key}`) and `skin.obj` (MHR LOD 0, 73,639 vertices, 147,274 triangles, single wall, every edge shared by exactly two triangles).

Two steps: `fit_skin.py` does the parametric fit (the male to his own skin, the female to girth targets at 1.606 m / BMI 21.6); `enclose_skin.py` then pushes the skin out locally along its normals until every deep part (the pipeline's FAT_DEEP_TISSUES, integumentary system excluded) lies at least 2.5 mm under it.

### What is still wrong

- Female: her internals do not fit inside a BMI 21.6 skin. After the local push the skin encloses everything, but volume BMI rises from 21.6 to 24.7, and muscle outlines print through as lumps at the neck, clavicular region, forearms, knees, hands and around the orbits. This is not a finished body surface.
- The female's head is still tilted back (it follows her skull position).
- Female breasts: the HRA parts are not deep parts and the skin was not made to enclose them; 44–53 % of gland/fat/ligament samples are outside the skin and the nipple tips are 25–29 mm outside.
- Male: the external genitalia are covered by a mound pushed out of the smooth skin; it is a faceted bulge, not penile or scrotal skin.
- Both: the toes are one block (MHR has no separate toes); at the distal phalanges of the fingers the skin was pushed out by up to 39–44 mm.
- Samples still outside after the correction: three parts on the male (orbicularis oculi, at most 0.4 mm), two on the female (frontalis, 0.1 mm).
- My chest-girth measure is unstable on these two meshes (1059 and 918 mm on the male before and after); do not use it.
- The fingerprint cannot be recomputed from the bounding boxes in anatomy.json (the pipeline uses unquantised bone meshes); `fittedTo.key` is copied from `pipeline-report.json`, with a check that the export is newer than anatomy.json.
