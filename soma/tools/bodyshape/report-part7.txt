### 5. 中心型体型场

加了：各包里的 shape-central.f32，float32[n][3]，单位是“中心型控制量每 +1 SD 的位移（米）”，顶点顺序同 skin.obj；用法 skin += centralBuild × central；shape-fields.json 的 fields 里有说明，rig-shape.json 的 fields.central 指向它。做法：anthro-model 里 ANSUR 围度的第二个因子载荷给出各围度每 +1 SD 的变化，在 MHR 身份分量 1–6 里解（第 2 分量和体积 BMI 不动）。
它确实弱：女性要求的围度变化是胸 +3.5 %、腰 +4.2 %、大腿根 -1.9 %、大腿下段 -3.4 %、踝 -2.7 %，线性模型得到 +3.3 / +3.0 / -3.0 / -2.0 / -0.6 %，位移最大 10 mm；男性几乎只有腰 +3.7 %（得到 +2.1 %），位移最大 5 mm。NHANES 的围度没有因子载荷，所以臀、小腿、上臂不在约束里。
“降低身高时脂肪穿出皮肤”那一条我没有处理：它是运行时脂肪按距离场、皮肤按 MHR 权重两套做法之间的不一致，不在皮肤包里。

### 5. Central-build field

Added: shape-central.f32 in each package, float32[n][3], displacement in metres per +1 SD of the central-build control, vertex order of skin.obj; use skin += centralBuild * central; described under fields in shape-fields.json, and fields.central in rig-shape.json points to it. Method: the second factor loadings of the ANSUR girths in the anthro model give each girth's change per +1 SD; that is solved in MHR identity components 1-6 (component 2 and the volume BMI held).
It is weak indeed: for the female the girth changes asked for are chest +3.5 %, waist +4.2 %, proximal thigh -1.9 %, lower thigh -3.4 %, ankle -2.7 %, and the linear model gives +3.3 / +3.0 / -3.0 / -2.0 / -0.6 %, with a largest displacement of 10 mm; for the male almost only the waist, +3.7 % (obtained +2.1 %), largest displacement 5 mm. The NHANES girths carry no factor loadings, so buttock, calf and upper arm are not constrained.
I did not address "fat comes through the skin when stature is lowered": that is a mismatch between two runtime methods (fat following a distance field, skin following MHR weights) and is not in the skin package.
