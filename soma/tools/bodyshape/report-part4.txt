### What is still wrong

- The surface is "her deep parts plus a layer of the standard thickness for each region", so the build follows her muscles: broad shoulders, straight waist, with pectoral and knee relief still visible — a lean, male-patterned body, not a female fat distribution. - `compose_female.py`: the per-vertex correction is low-passed (otherwise every muscle bundle, and the facial muscles, print through); over the breasts the difference "female-shaped MHR minus base" is added; the head gets no correction and zero head identity.
- `enclose_skin.py female`: local push until every deep part is 2.5 mm under the skin, displacement capped at 20 mm.
