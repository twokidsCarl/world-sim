### 1. The female target: what was done

- Identity: solved from the female girth targets at 1606 mm and BMI 21.6 (six coefficients), and solved again after the pose (skeleton scales change the girths), two rounds.
- Pose: joints to the rig.json centres (half the weight of the skin fit, small weight on the knee) while bone must stay 3 mm under the skin; fingers with per-segment length scales. The hip-height, spine-length and hip-depth scales are bounded at +/-0.6: unbounded, the first two ran off together to 1.5, which pulled the perineum about 7 cm down and thinned the top of the thigh (visible in the previous render).
