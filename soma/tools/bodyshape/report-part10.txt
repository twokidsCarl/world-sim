### 1. Where the gauntness came from (diagnostic-front.png / diagnostic-threequarter.png; a left, b middle, c right)
(a) identity at the prior mean, warp only: the worst - deep furrows beside the nose and on the cheeks, drooping mouth corners, a ridge under the jaw. (b) fitted identity, no warp: the smoothest, youngest and most female; but chin, under the chin and cheeks are 5-8 mm thicker than the table. Conclusion: the furrows come from the warp (drawing the cheeks in by 10-12 mm beside an unconstrained nose), not from the identity and not mainly from the rims of the protected regions.
The final combination is therefore the fitted identity (first coefficient -1.26, the rest within +/-0.7) plus a softer warp (sigma 45 mm, regularisation 0.35, two passes), with the transition round the lid regions widened from 5 rings to 9. Result (image c): no nasolabial or mouth-corner furrows, a rounded young face tapering to the chin; my judgement is that it now reads as a believable young woman, better than last round and than the installed skin.
