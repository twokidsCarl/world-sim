## 第九部分：女性面部的干净目标（female-face-target/，kind = target，不可安装）

### 0. 还不对的地方（先说）
1. 作为一张脸：没有了外推造成的棱和包，轮廓是一个下颌收窄、颏尖的成年人，比已安装的那张更像女性；但它偏瘦削、显老：鼻唇沟和口角外侧各有一道明显的沟，下颌缘下方仍有一道浅的线，两侧颞部和眶外侧有浅折痕（眼睑保护区的边缘），唇薄。我的判断是“可信的成年人，偏女性但不明显”，不是一张一眼就是女性的脸。
2. 唇比厚度表薄：上唇处皮肤到上切牙唇面只有 5.5 mm（表 13.4），下唇 8.2 mm（表 14.1）。这是因为我按你的要求让唇由牙列定：把皮肤的唇面放在她现有的 Lip 部件外 1 mm，而那个部件（男性来源）紧贴着牙。要按表做，就得让唇向前 6–8 mm，Lip 部件也要跟着移。这是需要你定的。
3. 鼻：鼻骨下端处皮肤有 7.8 mm（表 2.6），因为保留了 MHR 自己的鼻子；鼻根 7.4（表 5.9），眉间 3.6（表 5.2，偏薄 1.6 mm）。鼻宽 36.5 mm，没有来源可比。
4. 没有打开任何女性面部量度的正常值（搜到的一篇是修图后的审美偏好，不是测量值，没有用），所以比值只有骨作参考。眉线相对眶上缘的位置：没有来源，不给数字。
5. 两侧和颊部的厚度用的是不分性别、不分人群的 2023 T-Table；我没有自己再打开原文，引用的是知识库审计里记录的数值。正中仍用 Sonita 2025 的女性样本（柬埔寨）。
6. 包络的厚度规则是我的假定（见 §2），不是文献值。
7. 乳房和口裂的新数字（体积 325–340 mL、切迹到乳头约 19.05 cm、口裂在切缘上 2.5–3 mm）这一轮没有用，按你说的留到下一次整体重拟合；这个目标里仍是 370 mL / 19.8 cm / 2 mm。
8. 量度里“下唇高 26 mm”“内眦间距 27 mm”是按网格上的褶和标志点近似量的，定义粗，只适合前后对比。

## Part 9: the clean target for the female face (female-face-target/, kind = target, not installable)

### 0. What is still wrong (first)
1. As a face: the ridges and bumps of the enclosure are gone, and the outline is an adult with a narrowed jaw and a tapered chin, more female than the installed one; but it is on the gaunt side and looks older: a marked furrow at each nasolabial fold and beside each mouth corner, a faint line still under the mandibular border, shallow creases on the temples and beside the orbits (the rim of the protected lid regions), thin lips. My judgement: a believable adult, leaning female but not obviously so; not a face that reads female at a glance.
2. The lips are thinner than the depth table: 5.5 mm from the skin to the labial face of the upper incisors (table 13.4) and 8.2 mm at the lower lip (table 14.1). That follows from letting the dentition set the lips, as you asked: the lip surface of the skin is put 1 mm outside her present part "Lip", and that (male-derived) part lies right on the teeth. Following the table instead means bringing the lips 6-8 mm forward and moving the part "Lip" with them. That is yours to decide.
3. Nose: 7.8 mm of skin at rhinion (table 2.6), because MHR's own nose is kept; nasion 7.4 (table 5.9), glabella 3.6 (table 5.2, thin by 1.6 mm). Alar width 36.5 mm, with no source to compare.
4. No reference for female facial norms was opened (the one paper found gives aesthetic preferences from edited images, not measurements, and is not used), so bone is the only reference for the ratios. Brow line relative to the supraorbital rim: no source, no number.
5. Bilateral and cheek depths are from the sex-pooled, population-pooled 2023 T-Table; I did not open the paper again and use the values recorded by the knowledge audit. The midline keeps the female sample of Sonita 2025 (Cambodian).
6. The thickness rule of the envelope is my assumption (section 2), not from a source.
7. The new breast and fissure figures (325-340 mL, notch to nipple about 19.05 cm, fissure 2.5-3 mm above the incisal edge) are not applied this round; as you said they wait for the next full re-fit. This target still has 370 mL / 19.8 cm / 2 mm.

### 2. Deep envelope of head and neck, and the conflict list
Rule (assumption): d = local distance from the target skin to skull or mandible; the envelope lies t = clip(d - 2.5 mm, 1.5 mm, 6 mm) inside the skin, smoothed. Where the cover is thin, 2.5 mm is left on the bone for a thin muscle or aponeurosis; where it is thick, skin + fat counts as 6 mm. On the head t has p10 / p50 / p90 = 1.5 / 6.0 / 6.0 mm. Outside the head female-target/deep-envelope.obj is kept.
Conflicts (conflict-list.json, per part: share outside the envelope, p50 / p95 / max, and share and max outside the target skin): 126 deep parts of head and neck have some surface outside (58 muscles, 2 tendons, 38 organs and nodes, 4 glands, 9 brain parts, 10 bones, 5 cartilages).
  - epicranial aponeurosis 63 %, up to 12.5 mm (11.0 outside the skin); frontalis 36-50 %, 4.6 mm (3.1 outside the skin)
  - sternocleidomastoid 32-74 %, 14.6 mm (10.3 outside the skin); platysma 3-23 %, 8.1 mm (7.8 outside the skin)
  - rings of orbicularis oculi 6-87 %, up to 8.5 mm (2.8 outside the skin; the inner ring is not enclosed anyway); nasalis 59-86 %, 4.3 mm; procerus 24-26 %, 2.4 mm
  - parotid 17-44 %, 2.9 mm (inside the skin); 10 nodes, 14-88 %, up to 5.9 mm (all inside the skin)
  - zygomaticus major and minor 7-18 %, 1.7 mm; masseter 4 %, 0.8 mm; temporalis at most 1 %
  - not in the list (wholly inside the envelope): digastric, mylohyoid, geniohyoid, buccinator, mentalis, risorius, submandibular gland, thyroid and cricoid cartilages; hyoid 2 %, 0.2 mm
  - hard parts: frontal bone 10 %, 3.2 mm (1.7 outside the skin: the spot at glabella where the skin is 1.6 mm thin); nasal bones 16-17 %, 2.1 mm; zygomatic bones 2-4 %, 1.7 mm; maxillae 1-2 %; five cartilages including the nasal ones 35-84 %, up to 8.5 mm (the nasal cartilages may show, by the agreed policy)
So what the pipeline has to bring in is mainly: epicranial aponeurosis and frontalis (or the target is low on forehead and crown), sternocleidomastoid and platysma (the target neck is slimmer than hers), the thin muscles round the orbit and beside the nose, the parotid and the nodes. The muscles under jaw and chin are not outside: the ridge under the mandibular border last time was not pushed out by them, more likely by platysma and by the tightened skin itself.
